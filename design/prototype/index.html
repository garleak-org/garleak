Abstract

We derive an analytic estimate for the lifetime of gaps opened in thin stellar streams by dark matter subhalo impacts, in static potentials and in a potential with a rotating bar. Treating a gap as a density perturbation that is erased by differential phase mixing along the orbit, we obtain tgap ≈ πR/σv in the static case. For a GD-1-like stream at 14 kpc this gives gap lifetimes of 0.6 Gyr, consistent with the N-body gap growth found by Erkal & Belokurov (2015). Bar resonances shorten lifetimes by 20 to 40 per cent for streams with pericentres inside 8 kpc. Gaps are erased after 0.4 to 0.6 Gyr, so subhalo counts from older streams are incomplete by a factor that we tabulate for five potentials.
