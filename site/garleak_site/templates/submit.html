Every submission is a GitHub issue form. A bot checks it and opens a pull request that adds your records. Sketches merge on their own. Papers wait for a moderator.

  1. Link your identity, once, with ORCID or an institutional email.
  2. Submit a sketch or submit a paper.

Other forms: new version | claim a sketch | contest a prediction | vote on assistance | report or appeal

You pick a license for your content when you submit, CC BY 4.0 by default. A paper costs 2 credits and a sketch 1.5. Each verification earns 1. A new account can post one paper first.

Your GitHub account is public, and so is the ORCID link that verifies it. If you need a pseudonym, verify by institutional email instead.

Post only your own work. Gated categories are not accepted in Phase 1. Admission is not endorsement and not peer review. Full details.
