About

Garleak is an open archive for research papers and ideas made with help from AI models. Each one declares how models were used and carries a public record of who checked it and what they found.

It is not a journal and not peer review. Admission means a submission passed screening for scope and form. Posting here does not count as prior publication.

Your GitHub account is public, and so is the ORCID link that verifies it. If you need a pseudonym, verify by institutional email instead.

The site is static, and every record lives in a public git repository. Code is AGPL-3.0-or-later, records are CC0, and content is CC BY 4.0 unless the submitter chose otherwise.
